Title: MDRAP: An open-source sidecar to catch corrupted ticks, crossed quotes, and phantom liquidity before your algos trade on them

Most backtests and trading bots assume clean feeds until a crossed quote or sequence jump causes unexpected fills.

MDRAP is an institutional market data sidecar written in Python with a native C hot-path kernel:
- Enforces 15 deterministic microstructure checks (bid > ask, zero size, Welford 3-sigma price anomalies, duplicate detection).
- Never silently discards data: quarantined ticks are logged to an audit table with bitmask reason codes.
- Performance: Standalone C core does 44–61 ns/tick into shared memory. The Python pipeline handles in-memory events at ~8.3 µs p50.
- Tamper detection: Has an audit chain that cryptographically breaks if any row in the SQLite ledger is modified.

100% open source under MIT: https://github.com/Aryan-20-04/mdrap
Video demo & reproducible benchmarks in the repo.
