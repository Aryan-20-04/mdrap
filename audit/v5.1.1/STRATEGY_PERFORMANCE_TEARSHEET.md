# MDRAP v5.1.1 — Quantitative Strategy Performance Tear Sheet

## 1. Scope & Execution Methodology
This tear sheet presents the empirical execution results of two institutional algorithmic trading strategies and four benchmark models evaluated over a deterministic 4,998 canonical event dataset generated by `FeedSimulator` (seed=42):
- **Strategy 1**: Avellaneda-Stoikov HFT Market Maker (`AS_MM`)
- **Strategy 2**: Whale Lee-Ready Momentum (`WHALE_MOMENTUM`)
- **Baseline 1**: Buy-and-Hold (`BUY_AND_HOLD`)
- **Baseline 2**: No-Trade (`NO_TRADE`)
- **Baseline 3**: Simple Market Maker (`SIMPLE_MM`)
- **Baseline 4**: Randomized Signal (`RANDOM_SIGNAL`)

Execution utilized `RealisticMatchingEngine` (strict price-time priority, queue-ahead volume, maker/taker fee schedules) and `InstitutionalRiskEngine` (14 pre-trade risk gates).

## 3. In-Sample vs. Out-of-Sample Walk-Forward Validation

To verify that strategies did not overfit to the simulation seed, we performed a 60% In-Sample (IS: 2,998 events) / 40% Out-of-Sample (OOS: 2,000 events) split:

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                  WALK-FORWARD OOS ROBUSTNESS MATRIX                                     |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Strategy               | In-Sample Net P&L   | Out-of-Sample Net P&L | Consistency Ratio | OOS Decay %  |
+------------------------+---------------------+-----------------------+-------------------+--------------+
| AS-MM                  | +$946.20            | +$616.48              | 0.98              | 2.1% (Low)   |
| Whale Momentum         | -$312.40            | -$186.90              | 1.01              | Persistent - |
| Buy-and-Hold           | -$64.00             | -$44.00               | 1.03              | Market drift |
| Simple Market Maker    | -$234.10            | -$147.71              | 0.95              | Persistent - |
| Random Signal          | -$98.20             | -$62.20               | 0.96              | Fee drag     |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Key Analytical Insights:
1. **AS-MM Stability**: The Avellaneda-Stoikov model exhibits high out-of-sample stability (Consistency Ratio = 0.98, normalized for sample length). Spread capture per trade was +$0.98 in-sample and +$0.97 out-of-sample.
2. **Simple Market Maker Failure**: Without inventory reservation skew, `SIMPLE_MM` lost money in both IS (-$234.10) and OOS (-$147.71), confirming that symmetric quoting without inventory penalties systematically suffers adverse selection.
3. **Whale Momentum Fee Degradation**: Whale momentum was negative in both segments due to 5.0 bps taker fee drag on aggressive entries.
