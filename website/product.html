Deep-Dive: The MDRAP Reliability Engine

Engineered specifically for quantitative trading teams, algorithmic execution desks, and risk controllers who cannot afford silent data corruption.

CAPABILITY 01

Passive Multi-Venue Ingestion & Normalization

Unifies fragmented exchange schemas into a strongly typed canonical event stream.

Every venue pushes market data in its own proprietary JSON, WebSocket, or binary encoding format. MDRAP connects strictly in passive read-only mode to exchanges like Coinbase, Kraken, Binance, and Databento.

  • Zero API execution credentials or private secrets required.
  • Stamps precise tri-timestamp vectors: (exchange_ts, receive_ts, processing_ts).
  • Assigns deterministic monotonic IDs avoiding non-deterministic UUID overhead.
  • Preserves raw wire bytes and computes immediate SHA-256 payload digests.
// CanonicalEvent Schema
CanonicalEvent:
  event_id:             "evt-cd75491fa9b6-10"
  instrument_id:        "BTC-USD"
  source:               "COINBASE"
  sequence_number:      1049281
  exchange_timestamp:   1700000000.124500
  receive_timestamp:    1700000000.126100
  processing_timestamp: 1700000000.126130
  bid_price:            65200.00
  ask_price:            65200.50
  quality_status:       VALID
  payload_sha256:       "fedd0ea63dcb92..."
  lineage_hash:         "240057078e6226..."
CAPABILITY 02

15-Rule Deterministic Microstructure Engine

Evaluates every tick in sub-microsecond time with strict priority preservation.

Spread & Book Checks

Instantly flags inverted books (bid ≥ ask), extreme spread widening (>500 bps), and empty level 2 order book snapshots.

Sequence & Protocol Continuity

Detects dropped exchange packets by tracking monotonic sequence counters across venues, catching sequence gaps (≥ 2 packets).

Temporal & Network Health

Identifies clock skew (>50ms drift against monotonic QPC clocks), stale ticks (>3.0s quiet intervals), and future-dated frames.

Strict Priority Invariant: INVALID > SUSPICIOUS > VALID. Once an event is marked INVALID or SUSPICIOUS, downstream reconcilers can never downgrade its severity.
CAPABILITY 03

Cryptographic Lineage & RFC 6962 Merkle Anchoring

Mathematics-backed forensic proof that market data records were not forged or altered.

Multi-Field Lineage Hash

MDRAP computes a continuous cryptographic binding linking raw frames to validation outcomes:

SHA256(event_id : raw_id : provider : instrument : data_mode : payload_sha256 : quality_status : processing_timestamp)

RFC 6962 Inclusion Proofs

Audit batches and incident corridors are structured into Merkle trees using Certificate Transparency domain separation:

Leaf: SHA256(0x00 || LineageHash)
Node: SHA256(0x01 || LeftChild || RightChild)
CAPABILITY 04

Deterministic Replay & Standalone Verification

Reproduce market microstructure state exactly as it occurred during volatility anomalies.

Deterministic Incident Replay Engine

When a sequence jump or spread inversion occurs, MDRAP records the entire time corridor into SQLite WAL. Researchers can replay the corridor under an isolated VirtualClock, ensuring the order book and pricing behavior recreate bit-for-bit with 0 timing jitter.

python cli.py replay --incident inc-1700000001-0001

Zero-Dependency External Verifier

MDRAP ships with tools/verify_evidence.py — a 100% self-contained Python script requiring zero external packages. Compliance auditors and legal teams can verify exported evidence packages on air-gapped workstations without installing MDRAP.

python tools/verify_evidence.py evidence_inc-*.json
