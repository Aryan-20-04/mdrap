ROI JUSTIFICATION

The Cost of a Single Market Data Anomaly

Crossed Book Arbitrage Trap

During a sudden sell-off, a feed delays an order cancellation by 250ms, showing a crossed spread. An algorithmic execution algorithm fires into phantom liquidity, incurring immediate slippage losses of $15,000 to $100,000+.

Sequence Gap Pricing Distortion

An exchange drops 300 WebSocket packets during a burst. Without sequence tracking, pricing models calculate volatility against a 20-second stale curve, mispricing downstream derivatives.

Forensic Audit & Trade Disputes

Resolving an execution dispute with an exchange broker requires cryptographic proof of what wire bytes were delivered. MDRAP’s RFC 6962 Merkle proofs resolve disputes with mathematical finality.
