THE PROBLEM

Market Data Feeds Fail During High Volatility Cascades

When liquidity evaporates and order cancellation storms overwhelm exchanges, market-data feeds suffer silent packet drops, sequence gaps, crossed books, and stale ticks.

Silent Sequence Gaps

WebSocket packets drop during bursts. When prices jump 150 basis points across a missing sequence span, algorithmic strategies compute prices against phantom liquidity.

Crossed & Inverted Books

Stale order cancellations cause bids to cross asks across fragmented venues, triggering erroneous statistical arbitrage fills that destroy Sharpe ratios.

Non-Reproducible Incidents

Traditional logs record text lines without wire payloads. When a trade dispute arises, trading desks cannot deterministically reconstruct the microstructure state.

SOLUTION

Five Core Pillars of MDRAP Assurance

An institutional reliability sidecar engineered strictly as an independent observer.

1. Passive Read-Only Tap

Requires zero API secrets or trading permissions. Ingests public WebSocket and multicast feeds without ever touching order-routing pathways.

2. 15-Rule Quality Engine

Branch-free microsecond evaluation of inverted spreads, price jumps, stale ticks, clock skew, and sequence continuity with strict non-downgrade priority.

3. SHA-256 Provenance

Binds the raw wire message, normalizer version, receive timestamp, and validation verdict into an immutable cryptographic lineage hash.

4. Deterministic Replay Engine

Plays back historical incident corridors under an isolated VirtualClock, reconstructing order books tick-by-tick and verifying 100% bit-for-bit determinism.

5. RFC 6962 Merkle Proofs

Anchors incident batches into Merkle trees enabling compliance auditors to verify evidence using a zero-dependency standalone script.
