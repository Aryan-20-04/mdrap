## 1. Target Market Segments

MDRAP targets trading entities with non-trivial multi-venue market data infrastructure that experience silent WebSocket drops, cross-venue clock skew, and disputed fill pricing:
1. **Cryptocurrency Market Makers** (Mid-tier quants quoting dozens of fragmented venues).
2. **Proprietary Quantitative Trading Firms** (High-frequency arbitrage and market-neutral desks).
3. **Digital Asset Liquidity Providers & OTC Desks** (Counterparties requiring independent fill verification).
4. **Small Systematic Crypto Hedge Funds** (Engineering teams lacking time to build custom forensic auditing pipelines).
5. **Trading Infrastructure & Connectivity Platforms** (SOR and EMS vendors seeking neutral data quality sidecars).
